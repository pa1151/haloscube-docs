Quick Start Guide

Set up takes about five minutes: power on, join the cube to your Wi-Fi from your phone or computer, then open its settings page.

Before you start

What you need

  • Your Halos Cube and a USB-C power source (a computer USB port or a wall adapter)
  • A phone or computer with Wi-Fi
  • Your home Wi-Fi name and password. The cube uses 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi

Assemble

  1. Insert the display module. Slide it fully into the base with the USB-C port facing the rear.
  2. Clean the display. Wipe dust off the display surface with the included lens cloth.
  3. Clean the prism cube. Wipe all faces of the glass prism with the cloth.
  4. Seat the prism. Place the front edge down first, then rotate the rear edge down until it snaps firmly into the base.
Buttons on the right side, front to rear: Reset, Boot (not used day to day), and one reserved for future use.
Setup
1

Power on

Plug in the cube. After the splash screen it shows Set up your cube with a QR code, the name of its setup Wi-Fi network, and the password. This screen stays up until setup is done.

Each cube has its own setup network, ending in a four-character code that is also part of its web address later.

2

Join the cube's setup Wi-Fi

Easiest: point your phone's camera at the QR code on the cube and tap the prompt to join the network.

Or by hand: open Wi-Fi settings and connect to the network shown on the cube:

NetworkHalos-Cube-Setup-XXXX
Passwordhaloscube

The setup page usually opens by itself. If it doesn't, tap the "Sign in to network" notification on your phone, or open a browser and go to http://192.168.4.1.

Your device may warn "No internet" or "Not secure" on this network. That's expected: the setup page is served by the cube itself, and your Wi-Fi password stays on the cube.
3

Connect the cube to your Wi-Fi

  1. Choose your home network from the list
  2. Enter its password
  3. Give the cube a name if you like, for example Kitchen. You can change it later
  4. Select Connect

The page confirms the connection and shows the cube's address. The cube then restarts on your network, which takes about 30 seconds.

4

Open your cube's page

Reconnect your phone or computer to your home Wi-Fi. The cube's display now shows its name, its web address and a QR code for two minutes (tap the cube to continue sooner).

Scan the QR code, or open a browser and go to the address shown on the cube:

Your cube's address http://halos-cube-xxxx.local

If the .local address doesn't open (some Android phones), use the http:// number address the cube shows instead.

Keep it handy

  • iPhone (Safari): Share, then Add to Home Screen
  • Android (Chrome): Menu, then Add to Home screen
  • Lost the address? Unplug the cube and plug it back in: it shows its address and QR code for a few seconds every time it starts
Using your cube

Apps

Turn apps on or off and switch between them from the cube's page, or double-tap the cube.

Tap gestures

Single tap
The current app's action, such as feeding the fish
Double tap
Next app

On the setup screen a double tap skips setup, for using the cube without Wi-Fi. On the address screen any tap continues.

Settings

Weather

Open Weather's settings (the gear on the Weather app), enter your ZIP code and choose units. No account or API key is needed.

Name

Settings, then System, then Cube Name. The name shows on the cube's page and in the companion app, which helps when you have more than one cube. The cube's web address doesn't change.

Brightness and photos

Display Settings adjusts brightness, for example dimmer at night. Upload Photos adds pictures for the Photos app.

Updates

  1. On the cube's page, open Updates and select Check for Updates
  2. If a newer version is available, select Install Update
  3. Keep the cube powered while it installs (under two minutes). It restarts when done

Every update is checked for a valid Halos Cube signature before it is installed. If a release can't be installed on your cube, the Updates tab says why. If an update is interrupted, the cube keeps running its current version.

Halos Cube Companion

A desktop app that streams to your cubes over your network.

Now Playing
Album art and track info for what's playing on your computer. Tap the cube to pause, double-tap to skip
System Stats
Live CPU, memory and network use

The companion finds cubes on your network automatically. Its settings list each cube, with buttons to open the cube's page or feed its fish.

Help

Nothing happens when I plug it in

  • Check the cable and try a different USB power source
  • Press the Reset button (front, right side)

I don't see the setup network

  • Give it a minute after power-on, then refresh your Wi-Fi list
  • If the cube shows apps or its address instead of the setup screen, it is already on a network. Use the address it shows at startup
  • Press the Reset button and look again

I can't join the setup network

  • Scan the QR code instead of typing, or check the password: haloscube
  • Forget the network on your device and try again
  • Move closer to the cube

The cube didn't connect to my Wi-Fi

  • Make sure it is a 2.4 GHz network; the cube can't join 5 GHz-only networks
  • Re-enter the password; the setup page lets you try again

The .local address doesn't open

  • Make sure your phone or computer is on the same Wi-Fi as the cube
  • Use the number address (http://192.168...) the cube shows at startup, or scan its QR code

How do I move the cube to a different Wi-Fi network?

  1. On the cube's page, open Advanced and select Change WiFi Network
  2. The cube restarts into setup mode. Start again from step 2

How do I factory reset it?

On the cube's page, open Advanced and select Factory Reset. This erases Wi-Fi, app settings and the cube's name.

The display looks wrong

  • Unplug the cube and plug it back in
  • Check for an update on the Updates tab
